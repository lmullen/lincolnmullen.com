My colleague Stephen Robertson's incredibly detailed and thoughtful digital monograph, [*Harlem in Disorder*](https://harlemindisorder.supdigital.org/), has won the 2025 Ángel David Nieves Book Award for Best Monograph from American Studies Association, as well as honorable mentions for prizes from the American Society for Legal History and the Canadian Social Knowledge Institute.
