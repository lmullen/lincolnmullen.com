Part of the fun of collaborating with other people---and perhaps especially with graduate students---is that they think of things you never would have. So here is an [American Religious Ecologies](https://religiousecologies.org) zine ([PDF](https://files.lincolnmullen.com/pdf/relec-zine.pdf)), put together by [Shae Corey](https://www.shaecorey.com) who was a graduate student on the project. Shae put this together for a conference on DC history, and she also [wrote up a blog post](https://rrchnm.org/blog/how-to-make-an-academic-zine-for-a-public-audience/) for RRCHNM about how to create a zine. It's a useful and fun overview of the project, focusing specifically on identifying the congregations in Washington, DC.
