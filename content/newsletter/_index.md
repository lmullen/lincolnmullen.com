*Working on It* is an occasional newsletter about American religious history, digital history, and the making thereof. The newsletter contains a little something extra for subscribers, but mostly it links to recent blog posts, so it's not a bad way to subscribe to this website. (Older issues were standalone.)

There's an explanation of the title in the [first issue](/newsletter/1/).

The thing to do is to type your email into the box below and then confirm your subscription. When you subscribe you will receive the most recent issue. Your email will never be used or shared for any purpose other than sending you this newsletter.
