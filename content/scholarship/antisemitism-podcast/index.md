abstract: >-
  Antisemitism has deep roots in American history. Yet in the United States, we
  often talk about it as if it were something new. We’re shocked when events
  happen like the Tree of Life shootings in Pittsburgh or the Unite the Right
  rally in Charlottesville, but also surprised. We ask, “Where did this come
  from?” as if it came out of nowhere. But antisemitism in the United States
  has a history: a long, complicated history that is easy to overlook.

  <em>Antisemitism, U.S.A.</em> is a limited podcast series from RRCHNM, hosted
  by Mark Oppenheimer, that traces just how deep those roots go. Its ten
  episodes run from the colonial period and the early republic to the present.
  The series is written by historians John Turner and Lincoln Mullen, with
  Britt Tevis as lead scholar. It is executive produced by Jeanette Patrick and
  produced by Jim Ambuske.
